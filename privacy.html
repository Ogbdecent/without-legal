Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

What you give us

Your onboarding answers, habits, timers, check-ins, slips, and craving logs. If you create an account, we store these in our hosted database so your plan can follow you to a new phone. If you use Without without an account, the plan stays on your device.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google and managed with RevenueCat. We never see your full payment details. We receive subscription status so we can unlock Without Pro.

Analytics

We use PostHog for product analytics to understand which parts of the app help and which do not. We send event names and basic plan information, not the text of your notes or your identity statement. We do not use advertising SDKs or session replay.

Crash diagnostics

We use Sentry to collect crash and error reports so we can fix problems. These reports may include device type, app version, and a technical stack trace. They do not include your notes or plan text.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in Without or in your system settings at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run Without: hosting and authentication (Supabase), subscriptions (RevenueCat), analytics (PostHog), and crash diagnostics (Sentry).

Deletion

You can delete your account from Settings in the app. That removes your cloud data and clears your local plan. If you use Without without an account, deleting the app removes your local data. You can also email chefmeupai@gmail.com to request deletion.

Age

Without is not directed to children under 13. If you are under the age of majority where you live, use Without with a parent or guardian.
