Delete your account

You can delete your Without account and its cloud data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Without and go to Settings.
  2. Find the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes your account, cloud data, and clears your local plan. Earned trophies and history on the device are cleared too.

By email

If you cannot access the app, email chefmeupai@gmail.com from the address you used, with the subject “Delete my Without account”. We will delete the account and its cloud data within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, habits, check-ins, slips, and craving logs stored in our hosted database. Anonymous analytics events and crash reports may remain in aggregate form.
